# Campus reference sources

Retrieved 7 November 2026. These are historical reference photographs, mostly from 2021; they do not establish the completed state of buildings in 2026. Commons file descriptions and per-file licensing were checked through the Wikimedia Commons API before downloading. Only CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA files were accepted. No university, news, Google Maps or Bing imagery was downloaded.

Downloads use Commons-provided reduced-resolution versions (maximum requested width 1200 px; Commons may return a nearby standard width). Files were renamed only; no crops, retouching or color changes were made. Original embedded watermarks were preserved. The manifest records original/download URLs, metadata and SHA-256. For reuse, credit the named author, link the source and license, indicate any further modifications, and apply the relevant ShareAlike terms to adaptations. CC0 files do not require attribution, but provenance is retained here.

## Identity and dating cautions

- `main-gate.jpg` is an aerial view of the main gate and zero-point approach, not a measured elevation. The Mritunjoyi Mujib mural visible behind the gate is historical; do not infer its present survival from this photograph.
- `ibn-sina-historical.jpg` includes both the former Wazed Miah Science Building (now Ibn Sina) and the Applied Science and Technology Building. `science-faculty.jpg` shows the same complex. Match the footprint before assigning facade observations to an OSM object.
- `academic-building-2.jpg` is described by its uploader as Rabindra–Nazrul Arts Building; `arts-faculty.jpg` is the separate older Faculty Building. Faculties are organizational units and do not each imply a separate building.
- The Aisha hall photograph shows its entrance gate, not enough of the residential blocks to establish their height or facade.
- The 2010 field, pond-steps, garden and sky photos are especially old. Use them for surface/vegetation cues only, pending a current survey.
- Colors in photographs vary with exposure, grading, weather and repainting. Visible color is evidence for a starting palette, not measured albedo or a verified construction material.
