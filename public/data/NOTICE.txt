The bundled map.osm is an unchanged copy of the user-supplied OSM export.
OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The game projects and renders this data locally. Building heights, road widths,
building appearance, bridge elevation and flat terrain are estimates.
No Google Maps imagery, paid map API or online map tiles are used.
