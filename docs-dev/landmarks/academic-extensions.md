Real heightMeters stays null; heightEvidence reports provisional/low-confidence, measured=false. Courtyards/plan coordinates remain intact.

This deliberate opt-in is separate from direct height overrides: planned, disputed or unsourced counts cannot activate the envelope; OSM height/levels still take precedence. The separate Applied Science relation retains its OSM four levels. Mir Mosharraf/Rabindra planned targets remain inactive because completion evidence is weaker.
